How to Play

📜 Game Rules

Objective: Conquer all territories in the UK to win!

⚔️ Combat

  • Select: Click your territory (highlighted in your color)
  • Attack: Click an adjacent enemy territory
  • Requirement: Need 2+ armies to attack
  • Outcome: Based on army strength + resources
  • Losses: Winner loses ~30%, loser loses ~50%

💰 Economy

  • Resources: Generated each turn from territories
  • Rate: 1/10th of territory resource value
  • Armies: All territories get +1 army per turn

🎯 Territory Info

  • Number: Army count (shown in circle)
  • Name: Territory name (below army)
  • Details: Click to see full stats

Tip: Build up armies on border territories before attacking!
